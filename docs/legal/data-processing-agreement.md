# Data processing agreement (GDPR art. 28)

*Between the customer ("controller") and [COMPANY LEGAL NAME], [ADDRESS] ("processor").*

> Template — have it checked by a lawyer before offering it. It applies when a business
> customer's files contain personal data of others.

1. **Subject and duration.** The processor stores the controller's files in the controller's
   [SERVICE NAME] bucket for as long as the storage contract lasts, and deletes them as the
   terms of service describe after it ends.
2. **Nature and purpose.** Storage only: keeping, serving and deleting files on the
   controller's instructions, which are given by using the service.
3. **Data and people.** Whatever personal data the controller chooses to store, about
   whichever people. The processor doesn't look at the files except as point 8 allows.
4. **Instructions.** The processor processes the data only on the controller's documented
   instructions, unless EU or member-state law requires otherwise; it then tells the
   controller first, unless that law forbids it.
5. **Confidentiality.** Everyone at the processor with access is bound to confidentiality.
6. **Security (art. 32).** Data travels encrypted (TLS); each customer's bucket and access key
   are separate; the provider's storage is encrypted at rest; access keys are stored encrypted;
   access to the processor's systems uses strong authentication.
7. **Sub-processors.** The controller authorises: [Backblaze, Inc. / Wasabi Technologies] —
   storage, in [REGION]; Stripe — payments (no access to files). The processor gives at least
   30 days' notice of any new sub-processor, and the controller may object by ending the
   contract.
8. **Access to files.** Only where needed to act on a credible report of illegal content or a
   legal obligation, limited to what that requires, and recorded.
9. **Assistance.** The processor helps the controller answer data-subject requests and meet
   arts. 32–36, as far as the service allows (the controller can find, download and delete any
   file itself).
10. **Breaches.** The processor tells the controller without undue delay, and within 48 hours,
    of a personal data breach affecting its files.
11. **End of the contract.** Files are deleted as the terms describe; the controller can
    download them before then.
12. **Audits.** The processor makes available the information needed to show compliance, and
    allows reasonable audits with 30 days' notice, at the controller's cost.
